### Blockers

1. **`labelloop/harness.py:102–121`, `labelloop/look.py:111–122` — the IK gate certifies a destination, not the motion sent to Isaac.** The “lift” sets an absolute **EE** height (it can even lower the EE); the next chunk translates diagonally while rotating. Neither the via pose nor the return home is checked. Isaac need not follow the IK branch found offline. A right-arm view can pass while its approach sweeps a finger through the box, or while the two arms cross during a switch. Check the interpolated outbound **and return** paths with continuous-branch IK from measured joints and collision geometry for both arms, jaws, parcel and table. Park one arm before moving the other; abort on tracking error.

2. **`labelloop/look.py:82–87,111–122`, `labelloop/harness.py:73–85` — “5 cm fingertip clearance” is not established even at rest.** The check uses one nominal jaw-centre point, at the *requested* pose, against a square centred on a projected change-mask centroid with fixed 10 cm total height. A jaw can extend nearly 49 mm sideways; a tall or ROI-clipped parcel can extend outside the estimated box. Intersecting the ray at mid-height mitigates bias but does not bound it. Use both finger solids and palm at the actual opening, achieved FK, and a conservative parcel envelope including localization error. Decline the view if the envelope is unknown.

3. **`labelloop/look.py:143–157`, `labelloop/harness.py:78–82`, `labelloop/brain/state_machine.py:91–105` — pointing is neither fail-closed nor an isolated calibration mode.** If all pitches fail, the least-bad target is commanded anyway and its rotation error discarded. A readable label bypasses pointing or ends the supposed indefinite hold. A left-tip miss over an estimated parcel also conflates perception error with base offset and does not validate the right arm that actually performs LOOK. Use a dedicated bounded test against known root-frame markers, test both arms, compare measured 3-D tip positions, and refuse infeasible targets.

4. **`configs/task_qr_sort.yaml:38–40`, `labelloop/brain/state_machine.py:106–136` — the retry policy can exhaust the case before a placement.** Three unsuccessful inspections cost 24 cloud calls; a timed handover plus one full placement adds 30. At 8–10 s inference alone that's 432–540 s, before motion (32/15 ≈ 2.13 s/chunk), scanning or planning. HANDOVER advances even with both jaws empty. Enforce a monotonic case deadline, reserve time for delivery, and end/retry each manipulation on observed possession or progress, not elapsed chunks.

### Should fix before measuring success rates

5. **`labelloop/harness.py:36–46`, `labelloop/brain/state_machine.py:61–78,92–96` — decoding is not parcel-associated.** Either wrist can confirm a bin/background code even in SCAN; conversely a previously sorted text is suppressed even on a new parcel. Low kraft fraction also overrides a valid decode. Track the current parcel, gate detections spatially in the active camera, reset confirmation per parcel, and handle conflicting codes rather than picking one. Test repeated ZIP-label texts and a visible labelled reject.

6. **`labelloop/look.py:50–72,129–140` — expensive synchronous planning searches a narrow pose family.** Up to 26 candidates × 8 seeds × 60 iterations × 7 residual evaluations is ~87,000 FK evaluations per parcel, including candidates already excluded by separation. Zero small-box views at one height/radius is not evidence of unreachability. Benchmark wall time offline; precompute a calibrated reach map, prune before IK and search height/standoff as well as azimuth.

7. **`tests/test_look.py:33–78` — the tests validate algebra, not useful views.** Independent matrix multiplication is valuable, but duplicates the legacy geometry; an aggregate count can pass with a blind induction location. Aiming below the *centre* does not establish that a near-face QR clears the fingers or has enough undistorted pixels. Validate FK against the loaded asset and score rendered, per-face decodes by location and parcel size, including zero-view cases.

**Verdict:** (a) No hours on pointing as-is; do a short, isolated, fail-closed marker test first. (b) No extended LOOK runs until paths and geometry pass checks. Then measure incremental correct routes per minute against head-only scanning, not just accepted IK poses.

**Strategy:** Keep the specified left induction zone; right-arm observation there is not itself a defect. If placement within the zone is adjustable, optimize measured **read plus delivery** success, not viewpoint count. Keep head-first scanning and a cheap validated LOOK for sides; label-up staging changes the random-face task. A controlled push/spin is worth benchmarking for hidden sides but cannot reveal the bottom; test a scripted tilt/roll with reacquisition for that case. The clearest improvement is to avoid mandatory handover wherever one arm can deliver directly and use permitted, feedback-checked Cartesian manipulation for repeatable grasps/placements. At 30% per grasp, adding transfers is costly; 0/3 turns is little evidence for budgeting three long WALLX inspections.
