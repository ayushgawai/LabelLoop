**Blockers**

1. **The default search has a known false reject.** `look.py:show_plan` (`for f in cfg["faces"]`), `state_machine.py:~84–103` (`_search_next`), `tests/test_v8.py:~148–164`. Put a valid red-lane QR on the bottom: SHOW excludes that face, SHOWSET restores the original pitch, and four yaw pushes cannot expose the underside. LOOK is off and INSPECT has zero tries. The tests *expect* the labelled parcel to go to black. Worse, `big_presence: 0.32` skips SHOW, SPIN and LOOK altogether; the config itself reports package 2 at ~0.4. Low kraft fraction can also route directly to garbage. Separate **unread** from **confirmed reject**; use size to select a manipulation strategy, not to terminate search. On timeout, defer/log unresolved rather than claim “no label.”

2. **SHOW is open-loop manipulation, not a verified grasp.** `look.py:fit_parcel` (hull projection), `grasp_plan`, `held_low`; `harness.py:scripted_reply` (SHOW branch); `tests/test_v8.py:~40–53`. Every silhouette, including its visible front wall, is projected at a fixed 80-mm height. Package 1 is described as 152 mm tall: even the 150-mm push approach is below its top. A biased fit can select the wrong grip width, centre or contact depth. After closing, the harness advances through lift and views even with an empty gripper; the mock makes the QR appear from the *command tag*. Estimate height and top-face rectangle from calibrated multiview observations or known parcel dimensions. Require observed jaw separation/contact **and parcel co-motion on a short test lift** before rotating.

3. **Failed return planning commands an airborne release.** `look.py:set_down` (`drop`, `return r or drop()`). A feasible outbound path does not guarantee this return: for example, a `via` call stores no `q`, so early confirmation there can seed the return on a different IK branch. Transit checks also omit links, open fingers, bins and the other arm; the final retreat-to-home is not checked by `chain`. Prevalidate a supported landing from every interruptible waypoint, retain actual joints, and collision-check the swept robot and load. If tracking diverges, hold and replan; never open merely because planning failed.

**Should-fix**

4. **Search loses the parcel after moving it.** `harness.py:~79–94, 147–155, 193–202`; `state_machine.py:delivery_arm`. With no decode, SHOWSET targets the *reject arm’s* handoff; subsequent SPIN fits only the induction ROI, which need not contain the moved silhouette. Also `plan_look` runs only for `SCAN → LOOK`; enabling LOOK after SHOW/SPIN can therefore yield zero or stale views. Track the actual settled pose, retain it as the search ROI, and plan on **every entry** to LOOK.

5. **Confirmation is not parcel association.** `harness.py:~43–58`; `state_machine.py:_done, step`. Wrist decodes anywhere count, so a stationary bin label can win two frames; conversely, a head-view label on a lifted parcel projected over a bin is discarded. Globally suppressing `self.sorted` also blinds a second parcel carrying identical text, even after a failed delivery. Gate by the tracked parcel projection in each camera, not text history or a static bin rectangle.

**(a) Physical verdict.** The pitch algebra is sound: at pickup, `Ry(pitch) Ry(90°−pitch)` makes box x vertical downward, and roll zero keeps jaw y horizontal. Restoring pickup pitch at another yaw therefore lands the *model* flat. It does not establish flat pad contact, adequate friction against gravity and turning torque, or a rigid centred grasp. `grip_close: 0.3` is a position target, not a force specification. Measure effort limits, pad overlap, slip and landing contact across sizes/pitches. Face-normal alignment alone also does not establish visibility: the jaws contact the very `y±` faces being counted.

**(b) Fastest coverage to test.** While holding once, try a prevalidated **free-wrist camera** pose looking sideways/up at the missing side and underside; the bottom need not point at the head. Verify clearance, pixels per QR module and jaw occlusion. If blocked, use a controlled supported quarter-turn onto a side, withdraw the fingers, and image the former bottom and newly unmasked contact face. Flat yaw-only set-down helps a missing side, never the bottom. Prefer this to an airborne two-arm transfer; validate a separate supported tipping maneuver for over-width parcels. Test actual rendered labels on all six faces, not orientation tags.

**(c) Timing.** No defensible sub-minute bound with this cloud flow. On a hidden label, six SCAN chunks + 19 SHOW/SHOWSET + two HOME + two VERIFY already cost **~62 s** at 32/15 s each, before delivery, IDLE or online IK planning. Each serial cloud action adds ~7–22 s including motion, with retries likely. Decode consecutive camera frames *within* motion/settling chunks, cache validated paths, stop on associated confirmation, and deliver directly from the verified hold when reach permits; otherwise make one supported handoff. Reserve local WALLX for exceptions. Measure arrival-to-verified-bin median and p95, including planning and failures; `state_machine.py:_cost` charges configured estimates, not elapsed time.
